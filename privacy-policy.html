Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

Sanvila Unisex Salon & Spa (“Sanvila”, “we”, “us” or “our”) respects your privacy. This policy explains how we handle information you provide when you enquire about or book our salon and spa services.

Information we collect

Depending on how you contact us, we may receive your name, phone number, email address (if you provide one), preferred service, booking preferences and the contents of your enquiry. This information may be submitted through our website, Google Ads lead forms, phone calls or WhatsApp.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your questions and service enquiries.
  • To discuss service options, prices, availability and booking requests.
  • To contact you about the enquiry or booking you requested.
  • To maintain business records and meet applicable legal obligations.

Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal information. We may process information through communication, website or advertising platforms you choose to use (such as Google or WhatsApp) and disclose it where required by law or necessary to protect our legal rights.

Security and retention

We take reasonable steps to protect information against unauthorised access, use or disclosure. We keep information only for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to your enquiry, manage our business records or meet legal requirements.

Your choices and requests

You may contact us to ask about the personal information you have provided or request its correction or deletion. We will consider and respond to requests in accordance with applicable law and any record-retention requirements.

Third-party services

If you use Google, WhatsApp or another third-party platform to contact us, that platform may process information under its own privacy policy and terms. Please review the relevant platform policy for details.
